# hcmc-land-cover
# Land cover of Ho Chi Minh City

Land cover map of Ho Chi Minh City within its boundary from July 2025
(former Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong and Ba Ria - Vung Tau),
using ESA WorldCover 2021 at 10 m resolution.

## Study area

Ho Chi Minh City within its boundary from 1 July 2025: the former Ho Chi Minh City,
Binh Duong and Ba Ria - Vung Tau, about 6,709 km² (GADM 4.1 province boundaries).

**Mainland only.** The Con Dao special zone (16 islands, about 76 km², ~230 km offshore)
is excluded. It covers about 1% of the city's area and is not included in the GADM boundaries.
